## OpenMap travel and entrance data

MapPins.lua contains dock, tower and dungeon/raid entrance positions, names and
level ranges taken from OpenMap's Data/Forever/Places.lua (which OpenMap builds
from the game's tables). Only the numbers and names are used; no OpenMap code.
Source: https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap
